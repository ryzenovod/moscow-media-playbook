Where to Shoot for Free in Moscow

City media hubs and university TV studios with cameras, lighting, and sound.

«Molodezh Moskvy» Media Studios

Free city-funded creative spaces · Open daily 10:00–20:00 · Ages 14–35

Operated by the Moscow City Youth Project. They provide equipped video studios with lighting rigs, sound-isolated podcast booths, and editing workstations with Adobe Creative Cloud. Accessible without academic bureaucracy.

Hub A: Tulskaya Metro (Ryabovskaya Manufactory)

Kholodilny Pereulok, 3. Includes video studio and podcast recording booths.

Open on Yandex Maps
Hub B: Prospekt Vernadskogo («Zvyozdny»)

Prospekt Vernadskogo, 14. Video studio, sound isolation booth, and editing suites.

Yandex Maps
Booking procedure and reservation support
  1. Choose a preferred time slot and room on mosmolodezh.ru (Video Studio, Sound Booth, or Edit Suite).
  2. If the registration portal requires a Russian ID or Gosuslugi, I can book the slot under my student account in a couple of minutes and list your name as co-creator.
  3. Bring your passport on the day of the shoot to pass the front reception desk.

HSE Media Center (Khitrovsky Lane)

Full broadcast television studio, cyclorama green screen, multi-camera lighting setups, and sound recording rooms.

Official studio bookings require project affiliation. We can submit our joint video series through HSE Creative Extracurriculars (Креативная Внеучебка ФКИ) to reserve studio hours and bring student crew members on board.
