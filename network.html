Media Contacts & TV Bureaus in Moscow

Direct contacts for editors, correspondents, and Middle East research centers.

Why Direct Editor Outreach Works Better than Job Boards

Submitting general applications through job portals often leads to automatic rejections because standard HR software flags non-Russian diplomas and work authorization requirements. Editorial teams, however, prioritize broadcast experience, showreels, and language skills (Kurdish, Arabic, and English).

Automated HR Portals
  • Automated filters frequently reject foreign academic credentials without Russian notarization.
  • Entry-level HR coordinators often avoid quota paperwork for foreign applicants.
  • Applications sent to generic info-inboxes rarely reach commissioning editors.
Result: High rejection rate regardless of TV experience.
Direct Editorial Contact
  • News desks and business councils actively seek commentators fluent in Kurdish and Arabic.
  • Executive producers make hiring decisions based on previous broadcast work and recommendations.
  • Editors can commission work directly through standard freelance civil service contracts (договор ГПХ).
Result: Direct pilot tapings, paid commentary, and article commissions.

Editorial tip: Russian media outlets hire contributors through Civil Law Contracts (договор ГПХ). Taxes are withheld at source (13% for tax residents, 30% for non-residents during their first 183 days). Registering as Self-Employed (Самозанятый via "Мой налог") lowers withholding to 6% on commercial editorial contracts.

KRG Representation & Kurdish Media Bureaus (Mayakovskaya)

The Kurdistan Regional Government (KRG) Representation in Moscow is the diplomatic and media hub coordinating relations with Russia. Both Kurdistan 24 (correspondents Khoshawi Mohammed & Hemin Tahir Mohammed) and Rudaw maintain accredited bureaus in Moscow. The office also works closely with the Russian-Iraqi Business Council at the Russian Chamber of Commerce and Industry (ТПП РФ).
